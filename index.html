    COMMON QUESTIONS

    Google SERP preview questions

    Understand what a snippet preview can and cannot tell you.

    What is a Google SERP preview tool?

    It shows an approximate preview of how a page title, URL, and meta description might appear in a Google text search result. It helps you edit the wording before publishing.

    Will Google display my exact SEO title and meta description?

    Not necessarily. Google may create a different title link or snippet using your page content, the search query, and other signals. This preview is not a guarantee of what searchers will see.

    Does this tool check my live website or store my text?

    The preview runs entirely in your browser. It does not fetch your page, check its index status, or transmit your input. If you explicitly enable Remember this draft, the text is saved in local browser storage until you clear it or disable that option.

    Does this predict AI search answers or rankings?

    No. This is a conventional search snippet editing aid, not an AEO or GEO score, AI citation predictor, or ranking checker. Accurate content, crawlability and useful page information remain essential.

    Can I preview non-English titles?

    Yes. Unicode text and right-to-left scripts are supported. Character counts use grapheme clusters when the browser supports them; pixel estimates depend on available fonts.

    Is this an official Google tool?

    No. It is independently developed, free to use, and not affiliated with Google. The visual layout and editing guides are approximations.
