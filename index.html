COMMON QUESTIONS

Google SERP preview questions

Understand what a snippet preview can and cannot tell you.

What is a Google SERP preview tool?

It shows an approximate preview of how a page title, URL, and meta description might appear in a Google text search result. It helps you edit the wording before publishing.

Will Google display my exact SEO title and meta description?

Not necessarily. Google may create a different title link or snippet using your page content, the search query, and other signals. This preview is not a guarantee of what searchers will see.

Does this tool check my live website or store my text?

No. The preview runs in your browser using the text you enter. It does not fetch your page, check its index status, or send your title and description to a server.
